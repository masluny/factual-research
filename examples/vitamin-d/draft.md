# Vitamin D and acute respiratory infections

## Overall effect

An individual participant data meta-analysis found that vitamin D supplementation reduced the risk of acute respiratory tract infection [@martineau2017vitamin, p. 1 :: "Vitamin D supplementation reduced the risk of acute respiratory tract infection among all participants"]. Across 10 933 participants, the adjusted odds ratio was 0.88 (95% CI 0.81 to 0.96) [@martineau2017vitamin].

The most recent update, however, combined 40 placebo-controlled trials and found that vitamin D did not significantly affect overall ARI risk (OR 0.94, 95% CI 0.88 to 1.00) [@jolliffe2025vitamin, p. 5 :: "the intervention did not significantly affect overall ARI risk (OR 0.94, 95% CI 0.88 to 1.00, P=0.057; 40 studies"]. Large pragmatic trials were also null: a UK test-and-treat trial in 6200 people was not associated with protection against acute respiratory infection [@jolliffe2022test :: "This was not, however, associated with protection against all cause acute respiratory tract infection or covid-19"], and a trial of daily supplementation in older adults joined other recent null results [@camargo2024daily :: "recent trials, in different populations and using different regimens, have yielded null results"].

## Dosing

Earlier analyses suggested that daily or weekly regimens were protective (OR 0.81), whereas bolus doses were not (OR 0.97) [@martineau2017vitamin, p. 1 :: "protective effects were seen in those receiving daily or weekly vitamin D without additional bolus doses (adjusted odds ratio 0.81, 0.72 to 0.91) but not in those receiving one or more bolus doses (adjusted odds ratio 0.97, 0.86 to 1.10"]. The 2025 update did not confirm this effect modification [@jolliffe2025vitamin, p. 5 :: "Pre-specified subgroup analysis did not reveal evidence of effect modification by age, baseline vitamin D status, or dosing regimen"].

## Bottom line

The benefit, if any, appears small and is now uncertain; the certainty of evidence for an overall protective effect is moderate at best.
